How to Reset Your Password

If you've forgotten your password or need to reset it for security reasons, follow these steps:

Method 1: Self-Service Password Reset
1. Go to the company portal at https://passwordreset.microsoftonline.com/
2. Enter your username or email address
3. Enter the Captcha accordingly.
4. Click on Next.You can chooose verificiation models from the given options.
   1.Alternate Mail ID
   2.Text mobile number by otp.
   3.Call to mobile number.
5. Choose alternate email id 
6. Enter verificiation code from the alternate email.
7. Now you can create a new password.
8. Your new password must meet these requirements:
   - At least 12 characters long
   - Contains uppercase and lowercase letters
   - Contains at least one number
   - Contains at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
   - Cannot be any of your last 5 passwords
9. Click "Reset Password" to complete the process
